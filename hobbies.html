Things I Enjoy Doing

When I am free from university assignments, I spend my time on these activities:

01

Playing Futsal

I play futsal with my friends on weekends to stay active, build teamwork, and clear my mind after a busy week of classes. It helps me stay energetic and maintain a healthy routine.

02

Table Tennis

I enjoy playing table tennis with my friends. It is a fantastic way to improve my reflexes and engage in healthy competition right on campus during breaks.

03

Walking

I like going for evening walks at the beautiful parks around Islamabad. The natural environment provides a perfect space for reflection and clearing my thoughts.

04

Watching Documentaries

I spend time watching and listening to educational content, documentaries, and historical programs to broaden my perspective and learn new things about the world.
